- **Patterns collect automatically at run end.** `devteam run` now calls pattern collection itself, fire-and-forget, on a clean `pipeline-complete` and on any halt that left a gate on disk — closing the loop that previously required a manual `devteam patterns collect` after every run. Collection also now consults `retired.json`: a candidate matching a retired pattern's identity is dropped and counted as `suppressed` in the collection summary instead of silently re-entering the candidate pool. `devteam patterns collect` is unchanged for manual and backfill use. *Honest scope note:* `devteam patterns list`/`review`/`promote` still recompute candidates independently from raw observations and do not consult `retired.json` or `pending-review.json` — retired-key suppression only guards the auto/manual collection path, not those commands directly (tracked as an out-of-scope finding).
